Our Mission

Mission Medics is dedicated to supporting individuals living with disabilities and illnesses. By bringing new life to used medical equipment and assistive tools, we help make daily life easier, more accessible, and more fulfilling for the people who need it most.

Our Vision

Because we believe that mobility and independence are fundamental human rights, Project MEND is deeply committed to supporting those living with disabilities and life-altering illnesses. We provide thoughtful, innovative solutions to help clear the path of everyday obstacles, empowering every person to live a healthier, more confident, and fulfilling life.

Our Impact

For over three decades, Project MEND has been a caring presence for our community's most vulnerable members. Since our founding in 1992, we've had the privilege of helping thousands of individuals find the exact tools they need to navigate life with a disability, bringing comfort, dignity, and independence back into their daily routines.
